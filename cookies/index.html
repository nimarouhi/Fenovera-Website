Cookie Policy

How fenovera.com uses cookies and similar technologies.

Effective date: August 1, 2026

1. What Are Cookies and Similar Technologies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by a website you visit. They are used to make websites function correctly, to improve performance, and to provide information to the website operator. This policy also covers similar tracking technologies, including web beacons, tracking pixels, browser local storage, session storage, and software development kits (SDKs) embedded by third-party services, which may collect information about your interactions with our site in a manner similar to cookies.

Cookies can be first-party (set by fenovera.com directly) or third-party (set by external services we integrate with, such as analytics or advertising platforms). They can be session cookies (deleted when you close your browser) or persistent cookies (remaining on your device for a defined period or until you delete them).

2. Categories of Cookies We Use

We classify cookies into the four categories below. We reserve the right to deploy cookies in any category at any time. Before activating any new category, we will update this Cookie Policy and, where required by applicable law, implement an appropriate consent mechanism.

Strictly Necessary

These cookies are essential for the website to function and cannot be switched off. They are typically set in response to actions you take, such as filling in forms or navigating between pages. They do not store any personally identifiable information. We may currently deploy cookies in this category for session management, security, and form functionality. No consent is required for strictly necessary cookies under applicable law.

Performance and Analytics

These cookies allow us to count visits and traffic sources so we can measure and improve the performance of our site. They help us know which pages are most and least popular and see how visitors move around the site. Information collected by these cookies may be aggregated and is generally anonymous. Examples of services that may be used in this category include Google Analytics, Adobe Analytics, and similar platforms. We may deploy cookies in this category at any time. Where legally required, we will seek your consent before doing so.

Functional

These cookies enable the website to provide enhanced functionality and personalization, such as remembering your preferences, language selection, or region. They may be set by us or by third-party providers whose services we have added to our pages. We may deploy cookies in this category at any time. Where legally required, we will seek your consent before doing so.

Targeting and Marketing

These cookies may be set by us or our advertising partners. They may be used to build a profile of your interests and display relevant advertisements on our site and on other websites and platforms. They do not directly store personal information but uniquely identify your browser and device. Examples of services that may be used in this category include Google Ads, Meta (Facebook) Pixel, LinkedIn Insight Tag, and similar advertising and retargeting platforms. We may deploy cookies in this category at any time. Where legally required, we will seek your consent before doing so.

3. Third-Party Cookies and Technologies

Some features of our website may involve third-party services that set their own cookies on your device. We do not control these third-party cookies, and they are governed by the privacy and cookie policies of the respective third parties. When we integrate a third-party service, we will update this policy to identify the service and describe the nature of its data collection.

Third-party service providers we may engage include, but are not limited to: website analytics providers, advertising networks, remarketing services, social media platforms, customer support tools, and payment processors. Each of these may use cookies, pixels, or similar technologies in connection with the services they provide to us.

4. Your Choices and Cookie Controls

You have several options to control or limit the use of cookies on your device.

Browser settings. Most browsers allow you to refuse new cookies, delete existing cookies, or receive a notification when a new cookie is sent. Please note that disabling cookies may affect the functionality of some websites. Refer to your browser's documentation:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data
  • Safari: Settings → Privacy → Manage Website Data
  • Firefox: Options → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions

Opt-out tools. You may opt out of interest-based advertising from participating companies through industry opt-out tools, including the Network Advertising Initiative opt-out page (optout.networkadvertising.org), the Digital Advertising Alliance opt-out page (optout.aboutads.info), and for Google-specific advertising, Google's Ad Settings (adssettings.google.com). These tools rely on cookies themselves, so opting out on one device does not opt out on others.

Do Not Track. Some browsers transmit a "Do Not Track" signal. We will honor Do Not Track signals to the extent required by applicable law. Currently there is no universally accepted standard for responding to such signals, and our practices may vary accordingly.

If we deploy targeting or marketing cookies, we will provide a "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" mechanism as required by California law. Strictly necessary cookies do not require consent under applicable law and will be deployed as needed for basic site operation.
