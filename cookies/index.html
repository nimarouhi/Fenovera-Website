Cookie Policy

How fenovera.com uses cookies and similar technologies.

Effective date: August 11, 2026  |  Last updated: August 15, 2026

1. Overview

This Cookie Policy explains what cookies and similar technologies are used on fenovera.com, including those used by third-party services we have connected to this site. We operate this site for business visitors in the Bay Area and beyond; some of those visitors may be located in jurisdictions with specific privacy rights, including the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and the UK GDPR. This policy is written to satisfy disclosure requirements under those laws.

2. What We Store in Your Browser

Notice-dismissal preference (browser localStorage). When you dismiss the site notice banner, a preference is saved in your browser using the Web Storage API (localStorage key: fenoveraCookieConsent). This is not an HTTP cookie. It is stored locally in your browser, is never transmitted to our servers, and contains only the value "acknowledged" indicating that you have seen the notice. It persists until you clear your browser's site data. This is used solely to avoid repeatedly displaying the notice; it does not track you, identify you, or support any analytics or advertising function.

3. Search Engine Webmaster Tools

We have registered fenovera.com with the following search engine webmaster programs to monitor how the site appears in search results and to receive aggregated performance data (such as search impressions, click-through rates, and indexing status). These programs do not place cookies or tracking scripts on your browser through our site pages.

Google Search Console (Google LLC). We have verified ownership of fenovera.com with Google Search Console. Google's automated crawlers (Googlebot) visit the site periodically to index its content. Google may log technical request data (IP address, user agent, requested URL) as part of normal crawling operations. This data is processed by Google under its own Privacy Policy and is not linked to individual human visitors by Fenovera LLC. We see only aggregated, anonymized search performance metrics in our Search Console dashboard. Google's Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Microsoft Bing Webmaster Tools (Microsoft Corporation). We have verified ownership of fenovera.com with Microsoft Bing Webmaster Tools. Microsoft's automated crawlers (Bingbot) visit the site periodically to index its content. Microsoft may log technical request data as part of normal crawling operations, governed by Microsoft's own Privacy Statement. We see only aggregated search performance metrics in our Bing Webmaster dashboard. Microsoft's Privacy Statement: privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement.

Important note on search engine crawlers: Automated search engine crawlers are software programs, not human visitors. Traffic from crawlers is excluded from any interpretation of "users" in this policy. Crawler activity does not result in any cookies being stored in the browsers of human visitors to fenovera.com.

4. Meta Business Tools (Meta Platforms, Inc.)

We have registered fenovera.com with Meta Business Suite for the purpose of domain verification, which allows us to associate our business presence with this website. Domain verification itself consists of a passive HTML tag or DNS record that confirms site ownership to Meta; it does not install the Meta Pixel tracking script, does not set advertising cookies, and does not transmit information about individual visitors to Meta.

We do not currently load the Meta Pixel on fenovera.com. No remarketing, advertising, or behavioral data is sent to Meta from this site. If we activate the Meta Pixel or any Meta advertising SDK in the future, we will update this policy before deployment and, where required by applicable law, obtain your consent before loading that script. Meta's Privacy Policy: facebook.com/policy.php.

5. Form Delivery — Web3Forms

When you submit the contact or quote request forms on this site, your submission is processed by Web3Forms, a third-party form delivery service. Web3Forms processes technical information necessary to receive and route your submission, which may include your IP address and browser information, in accordance with its own privacy policy. Web3Forms does not set cookies on fenovera.com on our behalf. Your submitted data (name, email, and message) is used solely to respond to your inquiry.

6. Hosting — GitHub Pages

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., a subsidiary of Microsoft Corporation). GitHub may process standard server access logs — including IP addresses, browser type, and requested URLs — as part of infrastructure operations and security. These logs are retained and processed by GitHub under its own Privacy Statement and are not used by Fenovera LLC for analytics purposes. GitHub's Privacy Statement: docs.github.com.

7. Cookies We Do Not Use

At the time of this policy's effective date, fenovera.com does not use the following technologies, which are commonly found on commercial websites:

  • Google Analytics or Google Tag Manager
  • Meta Pixel or Meta Conversions API
  • Google Ads remarketing tags
  • LinkedIn Insight Tag
  • Microsoft Clarity or Microsoft Advertising tags
  • Any other behavioral analytics, session-recording, or ad-targeting trackers

8. Your Controls

You can clear the site's localStorage preference at any time through your browser's developer tools or site-data settings. This will cause the notice banner to reappear on your next visit. Standard browser cookie controls do not affect localStorage, but clearing all site data will remove it.

To opt out of Google's data collection across the web, visit adssettings.google.com. To opt out of Microsoft's cross-site tracking, visit account.microsoft.com/privacy.

9. California Residents (CCPA)

Fenovera LLC does not sell personal information and does not share personal information with third parties for cross-context behavioral advertising. The webmaster tools described in this policy do not involve a sale or share of personal information as defined by the CCPA. If you have questions about your California privacy rights, please contact us at the address below.

We will update this Cookie Policy before activating any new tracking technology or third-party service on fenovera.com. For material changes, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of the site after a policy update constitutes acceptance of the revised terms to the extent permitted by applicable law.
