Cookie Policy

How fenovera.com uses cookies and similar technologies.

Effective date: August 1, 2026

Short version: fenovera.com currently uses no tracking, advertising, or analytics cookies. The only cookies this site may set are strictly necessary for basic site operation. We do not use Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, or any third-party advertising or tracking technologies.

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files that a website places on your device when you visit. They are widely used to make websites function, to make them work more efficiently, or to provide information to the website owner.

2. How We Use Cookies

At the time this policy was last updated, fenovera.com does not use cookies for tracking, advertising, or analytics purposes. The site may set cookies that are strictly necessary for basic operation (for example, to maintain session state or security). These cookies do not collect personal information and are not used to identify you.

We do not use:

  • Analytics or audience-measurement cookies (e.g., Google Analytics)
  • Advertising or retargeting cookies (e.g., Meta Pixel, Google Ads)
  • Social media tracking pixels
  • Third-party behavioral tracking of any kind

3. Changes to Cookie Use

We may add analytics or other tools to this site in the future. When we do, we will update this Cookie Policy, revise the effective date, and, where required by law, obtain your consent before placing non-essential cookies.

4. Managing Cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to refuse cookies, delete existing cookies, or be notified when a cookie is sent. Refer to your browser's help documentation for instructions:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy
  • Firefox: Options → Privacy & Security
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions

Disabling cookies may affect how some websites function, though fenovera.com is designed to work normally without them.
