Cookie Policy

How fenovera.com uses cookies and similar technologies.

Effective date: August 11, 2026  |  Last updated: August 11, 2026

1. Current State of This Site

Fenovera LLC does not currently load Google Analytics, Adobe Analytics, Meta Pixel, Google Ads, LinkedIn Insight Tag, or any other analytics, advertising, or remarketing tracker on fenovera.com. This policy describes the actual current state of the site, not hypothetical future uses.

2. What the Site Currently Stores

Notice-dismissal preference (browser localStorage). When you dismiss the site notice banner, a preference is saved in your browser using the Web Storage API (localStorage key: fenoveraCookieConsent). This is not an HTTP cookie. It is stored locally in your browser, is never transmitted to our servers, and contains only the value "acknowledged" indicating that you have seen the notice. It has no expiry date set by us and remains until you clear your browser's site data.

This storage is used solely to avoid repeatedly displaying the notice to returning visitors. It does not track you, identify you, or support any analytics or advertising function.

3. Form Delivery — Web3Forms

When you submit the contact or quote request forms on this site, your submission is processed by Web3Forms, a third-party form delivery service. Web3Forms may process technical information necessary to receive and deliver your submission, including your IP address and browser information, in accordance with its own privacy policy. Web3Forms does not set cookies on fenovera.com on our behalf. For more information, visit the Web3Forms website directly.

4. Hosting and Security

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which may process standard server logs including IP addresses, browser type, and requested pages as part of infrastructure operations. These logs are not used by Fenovera LLC for analytics and are processed by GitHub under its own privacy policy.

5. Browser Controls

You can clear the site's localStorage preference at any time through your browser's developer tools or site-data settings. This will cause the notice banner to reappear on your next visit. Standard browser cookie controls do not affect localStorage.

6. Future Changes

If Fenovera LLC activates analytics, advertising, or other non-essential tracking in the future, this Cookie Policy will be updated before those technologies are deployed. Where required by applicable law, an appropriate consent mechanism will be implemented before any non-essential tracking is activated. We will not activate such technologies without first updating this policy.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy to reflect changes in the technologies used on the site or to comply with legal requirements. When we make material changes, we will revise the effective date at the top of this page and post the updated policy at least 30 days before changes take effect.
