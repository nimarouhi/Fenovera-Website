Aluminum vs uPVC vs PVC

Which material is right for your project?

Three common frame materials compared across the factors that matter most for your build.

Aluminum uPVC PVC
Frame sightlines Slim: minimal visual footprint, maximum glass area Wider frame profile, clean and uniform appearance Wider frame; can bow or warp over time
Thermal performance Good: thermal-break options available across all series Excellent: multi-chamber profiles provide built-in insulation Moderate: basic single-chamber construction typical
Maintenance Low: durable powder-coat finish, does not corrode Very low; never needs painting or refinishing Medium: prone to discoloration and surface degradation
Span capability Excellent: handles large openings and commercial spans Good: well suited to standard residential opening sizes Limited: not suited to large or structural spans
Best for Modern architecture, large glazed areas, commercial projects Residential builds where thermal efficiency is the priority Budget-constrained projects with standard opening sizes
