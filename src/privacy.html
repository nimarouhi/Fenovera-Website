Privacy Policy

How Fenovera collects, uses, and protects your personal information.

Effective date: August 11, 2026  |  Last updated: August 11, 2026

1. Who We Are

Fenovera LLC ("Fenovera," "we," "us," or "our") is a Bay Area distributor of window and door systems for residential and commercial projects. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect personal information through our website at fenovera.com (the "Site"), including our contact and quote request forms.

If you have questions about this policy or wish to exercise your privacy rights, please contact us at info@fenovera.com.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the information you voluntarily provide when you submit a contact or quote request form:

  • Name — required, to address your enquiry
  • Email address — required, to respond to you
  • Company name — optional, to understand your project context
  • Message or project details — the content of your enquiry or quote request
  • Project preferences — product type, timeline, and similar selections on the quote form

We do not collect payment information, government identification, or any special category of sensitive personal data. We do not operate any user account system. We do not use cookies for tracking or advertising at this time. See our Cookie Policy for full details on the technologies used on this Site.

We honor Global Privacy Control (GPC) signals transmitted by your browser. If we receive a GPC signal from your browser, we treat it as a request to opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information, consistent with our obligations under California law.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide solely to:

  • Respond to your enquiry or quote request
  • Provide product specifications, pricing, and availability information
  • Follow up on ongoing projects or orders at your request

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. We do not use your information for automated decision-making or profiling.

4. Lawful Basis for Processing (GDPR / UK GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, or another jurisdiction that applies a "lawful basis" requirement for processing personal data, we rely on the following bases:

  • Legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR) — to respond to business enquiries you initiate and to manage our commercial relationships. Our legitimate interest is providing timely, relevant responses to prospective customers; this interest is not overridden by your rights because you choose to contact us and we use your data only for the purpose of that contact.
  • Contractual necessity (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR) — where processing is necessary to take steps at your request prior to entering into a contract (e.g., preparing a product quote).
  • Legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c) GDPR) — where we are required to retain or disclose information by applicable law.

5. How We Store and Retain Your Information

Enquiry submissions are received via Web3Forms, a third-party form delivery service (see Section 6 below), and stored in our internal email communications. We retain personal information submitted through our forms for a maximum of three (3) years from the date of your last contact with us, unless a longer retention period is required by law or by the nature of an ongoing commercial relationship. After that period, personal information is deleted or anonymized.

We apply reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect your information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. However, no data transmission or storage system is entirely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share your information only with the following categories of trusted service providers, and only to the extent necessary to deliver their services:

  • Web3Forms — our form delivery provider. When you submit a contact or quote form, your submission is processed and transmitted to us via Web3Forms' servers. Web3Forms may retain submission data for a limited period consistent with their own privacy policy.
  • Email service providers — we use standard email infrastructure to receive and respond to your enquiries. Email providers may process messages in transit consistent with their terms of service.

All service providers are required to handle your information in accordance with applicable privacy law and are not permitted to use your data for their own marketing purposes.

We may also disclose your information if required by law, a valid court order, or a lawful government request, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Fenovera LLC, our customers, or others.

7. International Data Transfers

Fenovera LLC is based in California, United States. If you submit a form from outside the United States, your personal information will be transferred to and processed in the United States. We rely on the following transfer mechanisms where applicable:

  • For transfers from the EEA or UK: we rely on the European Commission's adequacy decisions, Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs), or the UK International Data Transfer Agreement (IDTA), as applicable, to the extent required by law when engaging service providers outside the EEA or UK.

By submitting your information through our Site, you acknowledge that your information will be processed in the United States in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

8. Your Privacy Rights

California Residents (CCPA / CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you have the following rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA):

  • Right to Know: request information about the categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected about you, and how we use and share it.
  • Right to Delete: request deletion of personal information we have collected from you, subject to certain legal exceptions.
  • Right to Correct: request correction of inaccurate personal information we hold about you.
  • Right to Opt Out of Sale or Sharing: we do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. No opt-out action is required.
  • Right to Limit Use of Sensitive Personal Information: we do not collect or process sensitive personal information as defined by the CPRA.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: we will not discriminate against you for exercising any of these rights.

To submit a request, contact us at info@fenovera.com with the subject line "California Privacy Request." We will verify your identity before processing your request and will respond within 45 days (extendable by an additional 45 days with notice if reasonably necessary).

Authorized Agents (CPRA). You may designate an authorized agent to submit a request on your behalf. To do so, provide written authorization signed by you (or a power of attorney under California Probate Code sections 4000–4465), and we may contact you directly to verify the request.

EEA, UK, and Other Jurisdictions

If you are located in the EEA, United Kingdom, or another jurisdiction with applicable data protection law, you may have additional rights, including: the right of access; the right to erasure ("right to be forgotten"); the right to restriction of processing; the right to data portability; and the right to object to processing based on legitimate interests. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@fenovera.com.
