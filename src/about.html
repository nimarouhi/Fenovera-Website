Who we are

About Fenovera

A Bay Area distributor of aluminum and uPVC window and door systems — built around product quality and technical knowledge.

Fenovera was founded to bring high-performance aluminum and uPVC fenestration systems to residential and commercial projects throughout the San Francisco Bay Area.

The Bay Area building market is demanding. Contractors, architects, and homeowners need window and door systems that perform — systems with the thermal properties, structural strength, and finish durability the climate and design expectations here require.

We source and distribute systems that meet those standards. Our focus is narrow by design: aluminum and uPVC only, evaluated and stocked because they are the right choice for the projects our clients build.

We work directly with general contractors, specialty glaziers, architects, and homeowners managing their own builds. We provide specifications, pricing, and lead times — and we know the products well enough to answer technical questions without delay.
