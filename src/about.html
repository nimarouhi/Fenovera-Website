About Fenovera

Fenovera is a Bay Area distributor of aluminum and uPVC window and door systems for residential and commercial projects.

Our offering

What We Do

We supply a curated range of aluminum and uPVC window and door systems, made available to contractors, architects, and homeowners throughout the Bay Area.

Our product range includes aluminum casement, slimline, awning, sliding, and tilt-turn windows; aluminum lift-and-slide doors; and uPVC hung, tilt-turn, casement, awning, and sliding windows, plus French and sliding doors in uPVC.
