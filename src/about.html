We source and supply quality window and door systems for residential and commercial projects across the San Francisco Bay Area.

Our story

Why We Exist

Sourcing windows and doors for a Bay Area project should be straightforward. In practice, it rarely is. Lead times are vague, specifications are hard to verify, and the people selling the product often cannot answer real technical questions. Fenovera was started to do this better.

We keep a focused, carefully evaluated product range. Before anything enters our catalogue, we review its performance specifications, build quality, and track record to make sure it is a system we would confidently recommend. We do not carry products we cannot stand behind.

Our clients get clear specifications, honest pricing, and accurate lead times. When a question comes up mid-project, we answer it. That is the straightforward service we set out to provide, and it remains the standard we hold ourselves to.

Our offering

What We Carry

We supply a curated selection of window and door systems suited to the demands of Bay Area residential and commercial construction. Products are selected based on performance, specification completeness, and long-term reliability.

Windows

We carry a range of window systems spanning multiple materials and opening configurations. Options include casement, awning, sliding, tilt-and-turn, and hung windows in profiles suited to both residential and commercial applications. Each system is selected for its sightline performance, thermal properties, and hardware quality.

Doors

Our door range covers systems designed for smooth operation, thermal performance, and clean aesthetics. Options include sliding doors, lift-and-slide doors, and French doors in configurations suited to interior-to-exterior transitions and feature openings in residential and commercial builds.
