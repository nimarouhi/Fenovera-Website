Opening type

Tilt-Turn

A distinct dual-mode mechanism: the sash tilts inward at the top for secure ventilation, or turns fully inward on a side hinge for full opening and cleaning. Popular in high-rise and European-style construction. Available in aluminum and uPVC.

Hung

Vertically sliding sashes: single hung (lower sash moves) or double hung (both sashes move). A traditional configuration common in North American residential construction. Available in aluminum and uPVC.

Hinged & French

Hinged doors that swing on a vertical axis. Hinged (single panel) and French (double panel, centre-meeting stiles) share the same mechanism and hardware family; the distinction is panel count, not opening type. Available in aluminum and uPVC.

Bi-Fold

Multiple panels that fold and stack to one or both sides, creating a near-full open wall. Popular for indoor-outdoor connection. Available in aluminum only.

Opening type · Doors

Lift & Slide

The handle lifts the panel off the sill seal onto precision rollers, then it slides effortlessly across a wide opening. Handles large, heavy panels that a standard sliding door cannot: tight seal when closed, smooth operation when open. Available in aluminum and aluminum-wood clad.
