# Fenovera — Marketing Video Script & Seedance Prompts
**Total duration:** ~75 seconds | **6 scenes × ~12–15 seconds each**
**Format:** Generate each scene separately in Seedance, then stitch in order + add voiceover

---

## SCENE 1 — Opening / Brand Impression
**Duration:** 12 seconds
**Voiceover:** *"Your home deserves windows and doors that match its ambition."*

**Seedance Prompt:**
> Cinematic slow drone shot descending toward a stunning modern Bay Area hillside home at golden hour. Floor-to-ceiling aluminum sliding glass doors open to a wraparound deck overlooking San Francisco Bay. Warm amber sunlight reflects off the glass. Lush California landscaping. Architecture photography style, ultra-realistic, 4K, shallow depth of field, no text.

## PRODUCTION NOTES

**Stitching order:** Scene 1 → 2 → 3 → 4 → 5 → 6

**Voiceover:** Record narration as a single continuous audio file, then sync to the stitched video. Suggested voice: calm, warm, professional male or female. No heavy accent.

**Music:** Subtle ambient instrumental — modern minimalist piano or light orchestral. Keep at ~20% volume under narration. Suggested search: "architectural brand background music" on Epidemic Sound or Artlist.

**Seedance tips:**
- If a scene has unwanted motion or characters look off, regenerate — Seedance varies by attempt
- For Scene 4 (macro product), use "image-to-video" mode if you have a clean product photo to anchor from
- Add "no text, no watermark, no UI" to every prompt to keep the output clean
- Target 1080p, 24fps output for each clip
